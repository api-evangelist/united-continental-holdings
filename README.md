# United Continental Holdings (united-continental-holdings)

United Continental Holdings, now known as United Airlines Holdings, Inc., is the parent holding company of United Airlines. It was formed through the 2010 merger of United Airlines and Continental Airlines, creating one of the world's largest airline operations. As a holding company, it does not directly offer public APIs — digital services and APIs are provided through its operating subsidiary, United Airlines.
